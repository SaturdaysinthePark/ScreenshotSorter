Privacy Policy

What the Screen? - AI Screenshot Organizer

Last Updated: January 28, 2026

Your privacy is our priority. What the Screen? processes everything on your device. We never collect, store, or share your data.

Overview

What the Screen? is a screenshot organization app that uses on-device artificial intelligence to automatically categorize and help you manage your screenshots. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information.

Data Collection

We do not collect any personal data. What the Screen? is designed with privacy at its core:

  • No user accounts or registration required
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No data transmitted to external servers
  • No third-party SDKs or services that collect data

On-Device Processing

All screenshot analysis and categorization happens entirely on your device using Apple's Vision and CoreML frameworks. This means:

  • Your photos never leave your device
  • AI processing runs locally using your device's Neural Engine
  • No internet connection is required for the app to function
  • Your screenshots remain completely private

Photo Library Access

What the Screen? requests access to your photo library for the following purposes:

  • Read Access: To find and display screenshots from your photo library, and to analyze them for categorization using on-device AI
  • Write Access: To delete screenshots you choose to remove through the app's review feature

We only access screenshots (photos with the screenshot media subtype). We do not access or process your regular photos, videos, or other media.

Data Storage

The app stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Category assignments for your screenshots (cached for faster loading)
  • Your review decisions (which screenshots you've reviewed)
  • App preferences and settings

This data is stored in your device's app sandbox and is not accessible to other apps. When you delete the app, all locally stored data is automatically removed.

Third-Party Services

What the Screen? does not integrate with any third-party services. We do not use:

  • Analytics platforms
  • Advertising networks
  • Cloud storage services
  • Social media integrations

Children's Privacy

What the Screen? does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The app is safe for users of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
